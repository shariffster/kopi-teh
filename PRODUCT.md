# Kopi / Teh — made your way

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

Web, desktop and touch.

## Product Purpose

Make a familiar kopitiam drink by pouring ingredients, stirring the visible mixture and serving a personally assembled cup. A carousel of premade Kopi-O, Kopi, Kopi-C and Teh recipes offers inspiration and a starting point for tweaks; an empty cup allows a free mix.

## Users

Common Ground Makeathon visitors and judges, curious makers and people who recognise the language of a kopi/teh order. The first interaction must be understandable within seconds and satisfying under the hand.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Full-screen photographic-style cup and real-time WebGL stirring with persistent ingredient colours. Add coffee, tea, water, sweetened condensed milk, evaporated milk or sugar. Liquid amounts in ml and separately added sugar in g; capacity300ml liquid and30g added sugar. Illustrative preset quantities, named with source-backed local order conventions. These are author-controlled recipe ingredients and a visual model, not measured taste or cooking chemistry. No accounts or uploads required. No fried rice in this build.

## Brand Commitments

Inherit Befrothed's warm stone, ivory ceramic, photographic texture, tactile liquid and restrained interface. Tiny serif wordmark, generous dominant cup. Controls support the cup; avoid dashboard or generic information-card composition. New name Kopi / Teh. Final arrangement selected from new MagicPath screen candidates, not inherited Atlas design.
