# Kopi / Teh — made your way

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

Web, desktop and touch.

## Product Purpose

Begin with a finished latte surface that invites an immediate stir, then deliberately make a familiar kopitiam drink by choosing a preset or empty cup, pouring ingredients and serving a personally assembled cup. The unchanged Kopi-O, Kopi, Kopi-C and Teh carousel offers cultural starting points. Optional froth controls let people shape the milk already in their cup.

## Users

Common Ground Makeathon visitors and judges, curious makers and people who recognise the language of a kopi/teh order. The first interaction must be understandable within seconds and satisfying under the hand.

## Capabilities and Constraints

A photographic-style cup with WebGL stirring opens on the exact inherited finished Befrothed surface. The Original latte state has a transparent illustrative ledger: 140 ml coffee, 20 ml condensed milk and 20 ml water. Those amounts are not inferred from the artwork. Opening presentation is intentionally independent of real or modelled fill depth.

Choosing an empty cup or one of the four cultural presets is deliberate. A visible drain precedes the new state; new pours show an artistic rising/recessed pool and ingredient surface layers. Reduced-motion draining takes 100ms. Add coffee, tea, water, sweetened condensed milk, evaporated milk or sugar. Liquid capacity is 300 ml; separately added sugar is capped at 30 g.

Heart, Rosetta and freehand froth controls shape the appearance of existing milk and do not add ingredients. Milk is required; preparation and serving constrain editing. Focus mode and its optional ingredient tray preserve the same drink. These are authored visual interactions, not measured fluid physics, taste, nutrition or chemistry. No accounts, uploads, purchases or fried rice are part of this experience.

## Brand Commitments

Inherit Befrothed's warm stone, ivory ceramic, photographic texture, tactile liquid and restrained interface. Tiny serif wordmark, generous dominant cup. Controls support the cup; avoid dashboard or generic information-card composition. New name Kopi / Teh. Final arrangement selected from new MagicPath screen candidates, not inherited Atlas design.
